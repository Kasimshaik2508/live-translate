Live Translate

Add your free Gemini API key

Live Translate uses Google Gemini to listen and translate. Each person uses their own free key, so nobody shares limits.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below.

Your key is saved only in this browser on this device and is sent only to Google. Don't share it with anyone.
